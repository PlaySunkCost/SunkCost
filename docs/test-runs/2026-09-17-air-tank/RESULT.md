# The air tank — 17 September 2026 — air MATRIX_PASS (73 rows), spectate MATRIX_PASS (210 rows)

Dan: an item on the site that looks like a small air tank; left click gives
50 % of the tank back (50 → 100, 0 → 50, 10 → 60, 70 → 100) and it becomes an
empty tank; two names, "Full air tank" and "Empty air tank"; players breathe
and throw the empty one on the ground.

## Built
- `AirTank` prefab (`DiveLootSetup.EnsureAirTankPrefab`, a copy of the
  basketball prefab like the coins): a 0.14 × 0.42 m capsule with a dark
  valve cube, safety yellow ([icon](air-tank-icon.png)), 4 kg, one hand, a
  slot, no value, use action `Breathe`. Registered in the prefab collection;
  two placements among the coins ("Air tank 1" near the landing, "Air tank
  2" out by the wreck), spawned fresh each day by the site's spawner.
- `AirTankItem` (on the prefab): a server-written `empty` SyncVar. Left click
  on a held item is the existing `ServerRequestUse`; for `Breathe` the server
  calls `ServerBreathe(holder)` — refused if empty or the suit is off, else
  `PlayerVitals.ServerAddAir(0.5)` clamped at full, and `empty` flips. Every
  peer derives the name, the use action (Breathe → Throw) and the look (the
  grey `AirTankEmpty` material) from it.
- `IItemTag`: the small interface `CarryableItem` asks for a name and a use
  action override; `PlayerBody` implements it too (the body's name), so the
  body-tag special case is gone.
- The visor's prompt while holding one: "Left click to breathe from the tank
  (+50% air)" / "Empty air tank — left click to throw it".
- `DiveSiteValidator` accepts a worthless air tank among the loot; the site
  scene was regenerated by the loot setup (the whole file reshuffles, as the
  builder does).
