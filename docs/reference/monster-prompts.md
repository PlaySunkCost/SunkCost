# Monster concept-art prompts (for an image AI)

Dan generates reference pictures with an image model, then models the monster in
Blender from them (docs/MONSTER_MODELS.md). Paste the GAME block and one MONSTER
block together. Written 22 September 2026.

## GAME (the same for every monster)

Concept art for a low-poly co-op salvage horror game, year 2300, on an ocean
world. Players are futuristic pirates — scavengers, not soldiers — who ride a
glass elevator down to a drowned human colony built over much older alien ruins.
Everything down there is dark, cold, silted; the only light is the divers'
headlamps. The tone is played completely straight: no jokes, no cute, no gore for
its own sake — dread, silence and things that are wrong. Art direction: stylised
and chunky like R.E.P.O. and PEAK — low-poly geometry, flat shading, few colours,
a bold silhouette that reads instantly in the dark from 20 metres. Not
photorealistic. Not a generic zombie or fish-man.

## OUTPUT (append to every prompt)

Output: a character sheet on a dark seabed background — full body front view and
side view standing (a natural stance, no T-pose), plus one image of the attack
pose. Clean silhouette, no text, no watermark.

## MONSTER blocks

### The Listener (2.0 m; clips Idle, Drawn, Hunting, Shooting; anchors BeamOrigin, Voice)

Design a creature called THE LISTENER. It is blind: no eyes at all, the face is
smooth or sealed. Its whole head is built for hearing — huge dish-like or fanned
ears, or a cluster of them, cocked and twitching. Tall and thin, about 2 metres,
stands very still and turns its head toward any sound. When it hears something
it fires a straight dark-violet beam from its head at the sound — show clearly
where the beam comes out (a mouth, a throat, a horn). It came from the alien
ruins: pale, deep-sea, something between a bat, an anglerfish and a statue.
Colours: dark blue-grey skin, one accent colour on the ears.

### The Lure (2.2 m; clips Idle, Drawn, Hunting, Shooting; anchors BeamOrigin, Voice)

Design THE LURE. It hunts light: it drifts toward a diver's headlamp and, when it
sees a lamp, fires a beam of bright light at it. Give it a lantern-like glowing
organ on its head or chest that draws divers in (anglerfish, moth). About 2.2 m,
pale, slow, moth-like. Show where the light beam comes out.

### The Long Walker (3.2 m; clips Idle, Drawn, Hunting; anchor Voice; Eye* meshes glow)

Design THE LONG WALKER. Very tall — over 3 metres — impossibly thin, long legs,
slow, and it never stops following you once it has seen you. Two glowing eyes,
no other face. A touch is death. A silhouette that is unmistakable at the edge
of a headlamp's reach.

### The Charger (1.4 m, wide; clips Idle, Hunting, Windup, Rushing; anchor Voice)

Design THE CHARGER. Low and wide, about 1.4 m high, heavy, a battering head or
horns, thick short legs. It shakes with a tremor before it rushes in a straight
line. Show the wind-up and the rush.

### The Weeping Angel (2.1 m; clips Idle, Hunting, Frozen; anchor Voice)

Design THE WEEPING ANGEL. A drowned figure, about 2.1 m, that moves only when
nobody is looking at it. Statue-like, barnacled, hands over its face or a hidden
face. Show it standing still and one mid-lunge.

### The Impostor (1.8 m — the diver's own model; a later card)

Design THE IMPOSTOR. It looks exactly like one of the divers — the same suit,
helmet and colour — but something is wrong: too still, too smooth, joints at
slightly wrong angles, no visor reflection. Show a real diver beside it so the
difference is the point.

### The Elevator Ghost (no body — the car itself)

Design the interior of a glass deep-sea elevator car lit green from inside, its
cabin light pulsing, doors about to slam. Nothing visible is wrong except the
light. Mood reference only; no creature.
