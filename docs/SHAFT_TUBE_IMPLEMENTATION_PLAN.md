# The glass tube around the shaft — implementation plan

Status: documentation only, 15 September 2026, branch `dan/shaft-tube` from
`main` at `1f00975` (the deck cabin ride, PR #25, merged). No Unity code, assets
or settings change with this document. Decisions in it are Dan's, taken on
15 September 2026 after riding the cabin; questions he answered are quoted in
section 3. Notion card: "Glass tube around the shaft".

## 1. Outcome

The car no longer hangs in open water. It rides inside a **clear glass tube**
only slightly wider than itself, from the deck cabin on the ship down to the
seafloor. **Water stands at sea level inside the tube** — dry above, sea below —
and that surface is where the cabin floods on the way down and drains on the
way up. A rider sees the water line climb the glass and their own body; the
F3 overlay says when the local player is under water. Nothing else changes:
the ride's stages, acks, locks and rosters stay as PR #25 built them.

Read [DESIGN](DESIGN.md) section 1 ("the glass elevator", "the way down"),
[NETWORK_CONTRACT](NETWORK_CONTRACT.md) section 9,
[WORLD_LOOP_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN](WORLD_LOOP_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) section 6
and [CONVENTIONS](CONVENTIONS.md) before editing. The ship-shell card ("Ship
shell in the dive scene") is a sibling: the tube's top passes through the
shell's hull and the water line is set against it. This plan does not depend
on the shell landing first; section 4.1 says what to do without it.

## 2. What is there today (facts, main at `1f00975`)

- `DiveSite01` is generated by `DiveSiteBuilder` (Idan's file, `SunkCost.Sites`,
  editor): `ElevatorAnchor_Top` at the world origin under a **surface platform**
  ring (hole radius `CarDiameterMeters/2 + ElevatorClearanceMeters` = 2.6 m,
  `PlatformSize` 20 m); the **seafloor** at y = −`ShaftDepthMeters` (45 m), a
  150 m ground with temporary boundary walls, the wreck, three dim point
  lights; `ElevatorAnchor_Bottom` on the ground at (0, −45, 0). Everything
  below is on the `DiveSiteDeep` layer, which the surface light and ShipAtSea's
  sun are masked off. There is **no landing room**: the car parks on the open
  seafloor and its doorway opens onto it.
- The **shaft** is open water with a 12 cm **guide cable** down the centre
  (collider removed in PR #25) — "no enclosing tube, just a guide" in the
  builder's own words.
- The **shaft gate** (`ShaftGate`, PR #25 version) is a solid cylinder the size
  of the car's footprint, 3.5 m tall, standing on the bottom anchor: it blocks
  and is drawn while the car is away, opens and hides when the car is parked or
  occupies it (`Progress ≥ 0.9`).
- The **underwater grade** is a local box `Volume` (PR #25) whose top is at
  y = −1 with a 4 m blend; fog and ambient are the scene's `RenderSettings`;
  the local player's camera clears to the fog colour while in the dive world
  (`WorldSceneFlow.PresentSky`).
- The **car** (`Elevator.prefab`, `ElevatorCabinBuilder`): 5 m across, 3.5 m
  interior, doorway at local +X, doors sealing over 1.5 s, a cabin light
  (PR #25). `ElevatorController` moves it between `topPosition` and
  `bottomPosition` **linearly** over `travelSecondsOneWay` (15 s, from
  `DiveSiteSettings.ElevatorTravelSecondsOneWay`), in its own state machine
  (local harness) or in the **driven mode** PR #25 added
  (`SetDrivenPhase(state, upward, elapsed)`, fed on every peer by
  `WorldSceneFlow.DriveCar` from `CrewDayState.Elevator` and the tick; the
  server's `ServerTickElevator` ends a move at `CarTravelSeconds`).
- The **deck cabin** on the ship (`DeckCabinBuilder`, in `Ship.prefab`): the
  same round cabin, glass shell + a second "car glass" ring (PR #25), doors and
  panel driven from the ride state; its floor is at deck height, y = 0 on a
  ship at y = 0, and ShipAtSea's sea plane sits at y = −1: **the deck is one
  metre above the sea.**
- The F3 overlay (`NetworkDebugSnapshot`) prints one detail line per network
  object from the **first** `INetworkDebugInfo` on it (`PlayerInventory` on
  the player).
- The rider is free inside the moving car; the car's per-frame delta carries
  the local player (`AddExternalMotion`); remote copies are placed from the
  server's `RiderPlacement` only while locked (PR #25).

Numbers used below: top anchor y = 0, sea level y = −1, seafloor y = −45, car
floor-to-roof 3.5 m, car radius 2.5 m.

## 3. Decisions (Dan, 15 September 2026)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Water inside the tube? | "The tube is full of water only below sea level — that's the point where water starts getting inside/outside of the elevator." |
| Where does the tube start? | Through the ship: the glass housing on the deck is the top of the tube. |
| Where does it end? | Down to the floor: the landing is the tube's last metres, the doorway cut into its side. |
| Size and look | Snug, clear glass: ~0.5 m around the car, the cabins' glass, thin ring ribs, the guide cable stays inside. Sealed: no way in or out but the two cabins. |
| Crossing the surface | Slow through the surface: ~1 m/s while the car's floor-to-roof span crosses the water line (≈3.5 s to flood), 3 m/s elsewhere; the same on the way up. |
| Who builds the geometry | Dan (me), in the builder, so *Create or Update Dive Site 01* regenerates it; Idan reviews. |
| Water look | A translucent surface plane at sea level (inside the tube and inside the cabin) plus the existing grade and fog when the eyes are below it. No custom shader. |
| Air, sounds | Nothing yet; the F3 overlay shows the local player is under water. |

Consequences recorded in the docs with the implementation PR:

- **Timing.** DESIGN and contract section 9 say 15 s each way "always". With the
  slow band the ride is 0.33 + 3.5 + 13.5 ≈ **17.3 s** (section 5). The
  invariant that matters — fixed timing regardless of load — stays; the number
  becomes "about 17 s, from a fixed speed profile". `travelSecondsOneWay` is
  no longer a setting; it is derived.
- **Water.** "Within about four seconds the water is over your head" and "the
  bottom door opens only when the cabin is full" hold by geometry: the roof
  passes the surface 3.5 s after the floor, 40 m above the bottom. The
  world-loop plan's `fillDelaySeconds` / `fillSeconds` / `drainSeconds` and the
  contract's "water level derived from elevator state and elapsed time" become
  "derived from the car's height against sea level". No sync state is added.

`DiveSiteSettings` gains `seaLevelY` (−1), `tubeClearanceMeters` (0.5),
`tubeRibSpacingMeters` (5), `travelSpeedMetersPerSecond` (3),
`surfaceCrossingSpeedMetersPerSecond` (1); `elevatorTravelSecondsOneWay` is
removed (derived, section 5). The validator (`DiveSiteValidator`) checks: the
tube exists with the set radius, the doorway arc faces the car's doorway within
2°, the gate leaves are closed at rest, the water surface sits at `SeaLevelY`,
the volume top equals it, no collider on the guide cable, no plug named
`Shaft Gate`.

### 4.1 The top of the tube

With the ship shell (its card): the tube rises through the shell's hull to the
shell's deck cabin, whose glass housing is the top — the tube's glass stops at
deck height, the housing continues it. Without the shell (this card first):
the tube rises to `CarInteriorHeightMeters` above the top anchor, i.e. the
height of the car's roof when parked, and the surface platform ring stays; the
top 3.5 m of tube glass then simply surrounds the parked car. Either way the
car at the top is inside glass, and the switch between the two is one
constant in the builder.

### 4.2 The bottom doorway

The parked car's doorway bearing is what `DiveSiteBuilder.CreateElevator`
rotates the instance to (toward the spawn point). The tube's doorway arc is
cut at that bearing so the car's open doors face the opening; the car's own
doorway width is 2 m at its glass, the tube's 2.4 m at radius 3 m. The
`TubeGate` leaves seal the arc while the car is away; the 0.5 m gap between
car and tube is not walkable from outside because the tube wall colliders
surround it — the only way in is through the car.

## 5. Motion profile (`ElevatorMath`, pure)

Depth `d` of the car floor below the top anchor as a function of elapsed
seconds `t`, piecewise linear, for a descent:

```
fast:  0 → d1            at v_travel (3 m/s),   d1 = topY − seaLevelY = 1 m     (0.33 s)
slow:  d1 → d1 + h       at v_cross  (1 m/s),   h  = CarInteriorHeightMeters   (3.5 s)
fast:  d1 + h → D        at v_travel,           D  = ShaftDepthMeters = 45 m   (13.5 s)
TravelSeconds = d1/v_t + h/v_c + (D − d1 − h)/v_t ≈ 17.33 s
```

The ascent is the descent run backwards in time: `DepthAt(t, up) =
DepthAt(T − t, down)` with the same profile (fast, slow through the surface while the roof-to-floor span crosses,
fast to the top). `Progress = DepthAt / D` feeds the existing `transform`
lerp. `ElevatorMath` also gives `WaterLevelInCar(carFloorY) =
clamp(SeaLevelY − carFloorY, 0, h)` and `IsBelowSurface(eyeY)`.

Where it lands: `ElevatorController` takes the profile (from
`DiveSiteSettings` through the builder's serialized fields, as it takes travel
seconds today) and uses `DepthAt` in **both** its local state machine and
`SetDrivenPhase`; `TravelSecondsOneWay` becomes a computed property so
`WorldSceneFlow.CarTravelSeconds` and `ServerTickElevator` need no change;
`WorldLoopSettings.CarTravelSecondsFallback` becomes 17.33 (it is only used
when the site is not loaded on the server, which the ride never allows).
The seal (1.5 s) is untouched.

## 6. Water in the cabin, the grade, the indicator (per peer)

- `CabinWater` (new, on the car prefab, `SunkCost.Diving`): a disc of the
  interior radius, the water-surface material, positioned each `LateUpdate`
  at `WaterLevelInCar(car floor y)` above the floor; hidden below 2 cm. Runs
  on every peer from the driven transform — no sync state, a late joiner sees
  the right level the first frame. The deck cabin never gets one (never wet).
- The grade: unchanged volume box; because the box top *is* the water surface,
  a rider's eyes get the grade exactly when they go under. The car's cabin
  light stays on under water (it is what lights the flooded ride).
- `PlayerSubmersion` (new, on the player prefab, `SunkCost.Player`, plain
  `MonoBehaviour`): `IsSubmerged` = the local camera's eye is below
  `SeaLevelY` while the player is in the dive world; `DepthMeters`; an event
  `SubmersionChanged(bool)` that nothing subscribes to yet (the air card and
  sounds will). It implements `INetworkDebugInfo` with
  `"underwater=True depth=12.3"` / `"underwater=False"`.
- `NetworkDebugSnapshot` joins **all** `INetworkDebugInfo` on an object with
  `"; "` instead of taking the first, so the player's line reads
  `slots=… held=… ; underwater=True depth=12.3`. `NetworkDebugOverlay` is
  otherwise untouched; nothing new is replicated.

## 8. Verification

### 8.1 Pure and asset (`Run shaft tube checks`, no Play Mode)

- `ElevatorMath`: `DepthAt` is continuous and monotonic, 0 at t = 0, D at
  `TravelSeconds`; speed 3 m/s at t = 0.1 s and t = 10 s, 1 m/s at t = 2 s;
  `TravelSeconds` ≈ 17.33 with the defaults; the ascent mirrors the descent
  (`DepthAt(t, up) = DepthAt(T − t, down)`); `WaterLevelInCar` is 0 with the
  floor above sea level, `h` with the roof below it, `SeaLevelY − floorY`
  between; `IsBelowSurface` flips exactly at `SeaLevelY`.
- Asset: everything in section 4's validator list; the car prefab has one
  `CabinWater`, the player prefab one `PlayerSubmersion`; setup applied twice
  reports no change.

### 8.2 Play Mode (`Run deck cabin ride matrix`, extended; Local host + one headless guest)

| Row | Expected |
|---|---|
| T1 down, sampled every frame | Car speed ≈ 3 m/s above the surface band, ≈ 1 m/s inside it, ≈ 3 m/s below (within 10 %); cabin water level 0 until the floor reaches −1, then rising at 1 m/s, `h` at the bottom; the local camera's `IsSubmerged` flips within one frame of the eye passing −1; F3 line contains `underwater=True depth=` below and `underwater=False` above; total ≈ 17.3 s; captures at floor-at-surface and eyes-under |
| T2 at the bottom | Doors open only with the water level at `h` (true by construction — asserted); the tube gate leaves open; the host walks out through the doorway onto the seafloor |
| T3 the tube is sealed | From the seafloor, a sweep of the player's capsule from outside the tube toward its centre (away from the doorway) is blocked by `TubeWalls`; from inside the parked car, a sweep through the glass is blocked; only the doorway arc lets the capsule through |
| T4 up | Water drains at 1 m/s through the band, is 0 before the top, dry at the swap; `IsSubmerged` false before the deck cabin; F3 `underwater=False` |
| G4 guest rides down and up | Guest snapshot gains `underwater=` and `cabinWater=`; the guest's values agree with the host's at the same tick within 0.2 m |

The existing R0–R5 and G0–G3 rows must keep passing unchanged (the ride
itself is untouched).

### 8.3 Feel gate (Dan, editor host, then Steam with a friend when a build is made)

The water line climbs the glass and your body; the ribs pass at a readable
pace; the slow band feels like sinking, not a stall; the bottom reads as a
lit glass room with a doorway onto a dark seafloor.

## 11. Risks the rows are there to catch

- The profile changes `TravelSecondsOneWay`; anything that hard-codes 15 s
  (docs, the harness self-check, `CarTravelSecondsFallback`) drifts — step 1's
  pure check pins the derived value and the matrix times the ride.
- The gate leaves must open before the car reaches them going down and stay
  open until it has left going up — the `Progress ≥ 0.9` rule from PR #25 is
  reused; T1/T4 sample a rider inside the car through the band.
- A translucent disc inside a translucent cabin inside a translucent tube:
  three transparent layers sort by distance, and the URP default can flicker
  where they nearly coincide (the cabin's water at the same height as the
  tube's surface for one frame). Keep the surfaces 2 cm apart and use render
  queue offsets if it shows.
- Volume blending: the grade must switch on when the *eyes* go under, not the
  feet; the box top at `SeaLevelY` with a 4 m blend already fades early — set
  the blend to 0.5 m and check the feel.
